Gentle reminders
At most one quiet message every couple of days, only when things look calm: few notifications, nothing on your calendar. No streaks, no guilt. Prototype stand-in for a proper Google sign-in. Needs the calendar.freebusy scope. Entries are saved to your private repo. With a passphrase they are encrypted (AES-GCM) before upload, so GitHub only ever sees ciphertext. Lose the passphrase and they are unrecoverable. Prototype only: keys stay in this browser's IndexedDB and calls go straight from the page. Move them behind a server before shipping. Without keys, Alive falls back to the browser's built-in speech recognition and a local heuristic analysis.
